  1. Step 0 — Admin registered Issuer A and Issuer B, their doors, and joint drop D1 done at start-up
  2. Step 1 — Issuer A uploads the attendance CSV; the fan claims from the inbox (OTP + wallet signature). A non-listed email is refused.
  3. Step 2 — Issuer B uploads a merch CSV; the row below $15 is rejected. The fan claims.
  4. Step 3 — The fan wallet holds both credentials at distinct addresses; a transfer attempt reverts.
  5. Step 4 — Door 1 scans credential A, name-checks, redeems and shows ADMIT after the receipt.
  6. Step 5 — Seconds later Door 2 rejects the same credential on AlreadyRedeemed, citing Door 1's tx.
  7. Step 6 — Door 2 admits credential B (its own issuer).
  8. Step 7 — Re-scan rejected; Issuer A revokes A2 and its scan is rejected; an expired QR is rejected.

Soft gate: issuers upload single-use presale codes to the mock ticket seller; the door is the hard gate.
